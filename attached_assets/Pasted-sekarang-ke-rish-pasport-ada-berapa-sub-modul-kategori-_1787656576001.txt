Menurut roadmap yang dilampirkan, Risk Passport memiliki 12 submodul utama, dikelompokkan ke dalam 3 lapisan.

Lapisan A — Foundation
Fondasi kebenaran dan histori Passport:

Passport Identity & Lifecycle
Identitas contract berdasarkan workspace, network, dan canonical address; termasuk status ACTIVE, MONITORING, REVIEW_REQUIRED, STALE, dan ARCHIVED.

Snapshot & Evidence Ledger
Penyimpanan snapshot Core Scan yang immutable, evidence hash, provenance, version, dan append-only timeline.

Risk Fingerprint
Profil risiko multidimensi:

control risk
trading risk
holder concentration
liquidity fragility
deployer risk
market/project risk
Reliability, Coverage & Uncertainty
Memisahkan:

risk score
reliability
coverage
uncertainty budget
insufficient evidence
Lapisan B — Intelligence
Kemampuan membaca perubahan dan konteks risiko:

Freshness & Passport Health
Mengukur umur evidence, expiry tiap kategori, stale state, dan health score.

Material Change Intelligence
Mendeteksi perubahan antar snapshot dengan before/after, delta, threshold, dan compound change.

Risk Trajectory & Momentum
Mengukur arah risiko:

IMPROVING
STABLE
WORSENING
VOLATILE
INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY
Control, Ownership & Relationship Map
Menampilkan owner, proxy, implementation, deployer, privileged role, serta membedakan OBSERVED dan INFERRED.

Distribution, Liquidity & Exitability
Menghubungkan holder concentration, liquidity, tax, restriction, dan read-only exit simulation tanpa menjadi rekomendasi trading.

Lapisan C — Action & Collaboration
Kemampuan tindak lanjut dan kerja investigator:

Evidence-Based Review Queue
Membuat daftar pemeriksaan berikutnya berdasarkan evidence, misalnya review owner privilege atau refresh liquidity.

Watchtower & Alert Rules
Monitoring berkala dan alert berdasarkan thesis yang dipilih user. Watchtower adalah submodul Risk Passport, bukan produk terpisah.

Comparison, Replay, Notes & Audit Package
Perbandingan Passport, historical replay, investigator notes, dan export audit package.

Pembagian delivery
Roadmap juga membagi 12 submodul ini ke dalam 4 fase:

Fase	Submodul	Fokus
P0 — Passport Truth Layer	1–4	Identity, snapshot, fingerprint, uncertainty
P1 — Change Intelligence	5–7	Freshness, perubahan, trajectory
P2 — Investigation Depth	8–10	Control map, exitability, review actions
P3 — Monitoring & Collaboration	11–12	Watchtower, comparison, replay, notes, audit
Hal penting tentang struktur ini
Ada 12 submodul fungsional, tetapi UX blueprint memiliki 15 bagian tampilan. Itu bukan berarti ada 15 submodul. Beberapa bagian UX adalah view dari submodul yang sama, misalnya:

Risk / Reliability / Coverage / Health
Uncertainty Budget
Why This Score?
Evidence Timeline
Comparison / Replay
Jadi struktur produknya sebaiknya:

Risk Passport
├── Foundation
│   ├── Identity & Lifecycle
│   ├── Snapshot & Evidence Ledger
│   ├── Risk Fingerprint
│   └── Reliability, Coverage & Uncertainty
├── Intelligence
│   ├── Freshness & Passport Health
│   ├── Material Change Intelligence
│   ├── Risk Trajectory & Momentum
│   ├── Control & Relationship Map
│   └── Distribution, Liquidity & Exitability
└── Action & Collaboration
    ├── Evidence-Based Review Queue
    ├── Watchtower & Alert Rules
    └── Comparison, Replay, Notes & Audit Package

Kesimpulan presisinya:

Risk Passport = 1 modul produk besar dengan 12 submodul, 3 lapisan, dan 4 fase delivery.

Core Scan tetap menjadi sumber evidence. Risk Passport tidak boleh mengambil alih logika provider atau membuat evidence baru secara diam-diam.
